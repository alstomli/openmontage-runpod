# Attribution and dependency record

This repository integrates the unmodified OpenMontage source from [calesthio/OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage), pinned to commit `d21f5d4eaa08422b09c7091757a05affcbabb35b` under `vendor/OpenMontage`. See `UPSTREAM.json`, `.gitmodules`, and the upstream source for its original notices. A copy of the upstream AGPL-3.0 license is retained at the repository root as `LICENSE`.

The serverless wrapper is separate integration work. OpenMontage's Remotion composer, Remotion dependencies, Chromium, FFmpeg, fonts, optional model/voice dependencies and supplied media retain their respective terms and attribution requirements. Model weights are not part of the source-code submodule.

Before a public/commercial release, review the actual shipped dependency inventory, renderer terms, selected models/voices and input/output media rights. Keep a record of their versions, source URLs, license files and any required notices. This notice records provenance; it is not a determination of legal compatibility or permission for every deployment.
